# OrderAI Demo Call — Restaurant Reservation (Friday Dinner Rush)
Duration: ~50 seconds
Voices: AI (warm professional female), Customer (American male, 30s, calling for two)

[AI - warm, upbeat]: "Thanks for calling Olive & Vine, this is Aria. How can I help you tonight?"

[Customer - friendly]: "Hey, can we get a table for four tonight at seven thirty?"

[AI - apologetic but warm]: "Let me check that for you... I'm sorry, seven thirty is fully booked. I do have eight o'clock for four, or I could seat you at seven at the bar. Which would you prefer?"

[Customer]: "Eight o'clock works for us."

[AI - confirming]: "Perfect — a table for four at eight tonight. Can I get a name for the reservation?"

[Customer]: "Daniel Brooks."

[AI - thoughtful]: "Got it, Daniel. Any food allergies I should flag for the kitchen, or are you celebrating anything special?"

[Customer - happy]: "Actually, yeah — it's our anniversary."

[AI - delighted]: "Congratulations! I'll have the table set with a little handwritten card for you. You'll get a text confirmation shortly. See you at eight!"

[Customer]: "Wonderful, thank you!"

[End of call - soft click]
